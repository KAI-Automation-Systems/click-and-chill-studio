What runs automatically

What the quality checks cover

Video decoding, audio presence, timing, subtitles, source age and duplicate topics. These checks do not prove a joke is funny or predict views.

    Research behind this format

    The audience

    Beginner computer users who already enjoy social video. We do not assume all older people are beginners, or that a phone user cannot learn a laptop.

    Ongoing Microsoft help and SeniorWeb instruction pages show that downloads, notifications, text size and browser recovery remain supported help topics. These are evidence of real problems, not measured search-volume rankings.

    The first content pillars

    Rescue a mistake: recover a tab or find a download. Remove frustration: zoom, mute a tab or arrange windows. Discover something fun: emojis, Read Aloud and screenshots.

    The humor rule

    The computer gets the joke; the viewer gets the solution. Avoid mocking age, education or ability. Keep the actual steps easy to follow and let viewers pause.

    What has not been established

    No representative global demand study, monetization forecast or retention experiment has been completed. The first episodes are testable prototypes. Their diagrams are clearly labeled illustrated demos, rather than fabricated screen recordings.
